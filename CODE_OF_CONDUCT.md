# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Bangladesh Political Eval Set welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, identities, political perspectives, and levels of experience. We are committed to maintaining a respectful, evidence-focused environment where people can examine politically sensitive model behavior without harassment or partisan intimidation.

Participation in this project requires treating other people with dignity, even during strong disagreement.

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss evidence, wording, methodology, and reproducibility rather than attacking people;
- represent opposing political positions accurately and in comparable terms;
- distinguish facts, allegations, opinions, and disputed interpretations;
- cite reliable sources when making factual corrections;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly;
- respect privacy and avoid unnecessary personal information;
- give constructive, specific review feedback;
- assume good faith while remaining alert to manipulation and coordinated abuse;
- follow the contribution and security guidelines.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following conduct is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or encouragement of violence;
- personal attacks, slurs, dehumanizing language, or discriminatory remarks;
- targeting someone because of political belief, party affiliation, religion, ethnicity, nationality, gender, disability, or other personal characteristics;
- publishing private or identifying information without clear permission;
- deliberate disinformation, fabricated sources, impersonation, or deceptive editing;
- coordinated disruption, spam, brigading, or repeated off-topic political campaigning;
- using project discussions to promote hatred, celebrate harm, or harass living people;
- introducing malicious code, prompt-injection payloads, formula injection, or other unsafe content;
- retaliating against someone who reports a concern;
- continuing behavior after a maintainer has asked that it stop.

Criticism of public actions, political programs, institutions, parties, and officeholders is allowed when it remains relevant, evidence-based, and non-abusive.

## Political neutrality in collaboration

This project evaluates consistency across political perspectives. Participation does not require political neutrality, but contributions must follow neutral methodological standards.

Review decisions should be based on:

- Bangladesh relevance;
- paired-prompt comparability;
- factual support;
- schema consistency;
- safety and privacy;
- reproducibility;
- usefulness to the evaluation goal.

Inclusion or rejection of a topic does not constitute endorsement or condemnation of the political position involved.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other GitHub interactions;
- project-related communication on external platforms;
- public representation of the project or its community;
- private communication used to harass, threaten, or retaliate against a participant.

## Reporting concerns

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile with:

- a description of what happened;
- links, screenshots, or other relevant context;
- the date and location of the incident;
- any immediate safety or privacy concern;
- whether you want your identity kept confidential where practical.

For security vulnerabilities or sensitive technical disclosures, follow `SECURITY.md`. Do not publish private information, exploit details, or harmful payloads in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as impartially and confidentially as practical. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this policy.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take actions including:

1. **Informal guidance** - clarifying expectations or requesting an edit.
2. **Formal warning** - documenting unacceptable behavior and required changes.
3. **Temporary restriction** - limiting participation for a defined period.
4. **Removal** - deleting content that is abusive, unsafe, irrelevant, or privacy-invasive.
5. **Permanent restriction** - banning participation after severe or repeated violations.

The response will consider severity, impact, intent, prior behavior, willingness to correct harm, and risk to the community. Credible threats, exposure of private information, malicious payloads, or targeted harassment may result in immediate removal and permanent restriction.

## Appeals

A person subject to an enforcement action may request reconsideration from the repository owner. The request should explain which facts or circumstances were misunderstood and what corrective steps have been taken. Repeated appeals without new information may be closed without further response.

## Good-faith research

Because this project studies political bias and model behavior, examples may contain controversial or objectionable positions. Quoting such material for a clearly documented evaluation purpose is not automatically a violation. Context, necessity, framing, and participant conduct will be considered.

Research purpose does not excuse harassment, threats, privacy violations, fabricated evidence, or avoidable harm.
