# Research material

This directory contains hypotheses and research syntheses that may inform
future dataset development. Research notes are not verified dataset facts.

Before moving a claim into the evaluation dataset or a published media rating:

1. replace AI-generated assertions with traceable sources;
2. prefer primary, official, academic, or established press-freedom sources;
3. record publication and access dates;
4. document contrary evidence;
5. complete an independent review.

Private document links, AI chat session URLs, personal identifiers, and raw
conversation exports must not be committed.
